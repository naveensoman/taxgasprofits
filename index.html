Stop making Australians pay a fortune to stay in Australia.

Stop racism at work before anyone has to complain.

Since 2022, federal law has made employers take reasonable steps to prevent sexual harassment. The Racial Discrimination Act has no such duty.110,116 In the federal public service, 77% of staff who said they'd faced discrimination of any kind didn't report it. Half of those didn't think anything would be done.144 In 2024-25, when the Human Rights Commission tried to settle a race complaint between the two sides, it worked 42% of the time, the worst rate under the four federal discrimination laws.145

Put the duty in the Act and let the Human Rights Commission enforce it, as the Commission itself recommends.110

Under federal law, employers must work to prevent sexual harassment. For racism, they can wait for the complaint form.

Ban outsourcing the government's own job.

The Coalition capped public service numbers from 2015. A Labor-chaired Senate committee found the cap pushed work to labour-hire firms and contractors. Coalition senators disagreed.146,147 Labor scrapped the cap in 2022 and set targets to bring core work back in-house. The Public Service Commission said the first year's target was beaten. The Auditor-General found agencies could meet the targets while spending more on outsiders overall. The targets were dropped in July 2026.146,148

Our law: if it's the government's own job, a public servant does it. Specialists, short bursts of extra work and independent audits are still fine. If a labour-hire worker fills a role for 12 months, it becomes a public service job. And every big contract gets published in full, fine print included, with only real security secrets blacked out.

A big new building needs Parliament's tick. In 2023, more than $3 billion of consulting and services contracts didn't.146

Most victims of domestic violence are women. Since the age of 15, 1 in 6 women and 1 in 18 men have experienced violence from a partner they lived with.130 Police must protect every victim. In that time he admitted he had lied. The state's police watchdog said police training and rules gave officers too little guidance on working out who is the main aggressor and who is the victim.150 Anyone wrongly accused, woman or man, deserves a proper investigation and a fair hearing. That doesn't mean arresting fewer abusers: arrest helps reduce repeat violence.151

Check the whole history, not just the latest incident. Let a court correct wrong records, with both people heard safely. And have complaints about police investigated by a body independent of police.149,153 NSW and Queensland have independent watchdogs, but both send most complaints back to police to deal with.149,154 Queensland's 2022 inquiry recommended a civilian-led unit to deal with all of them.
