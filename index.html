Why I started this

I grew up in the Gulf. Oil and gas money was everywhere: in the schools, the hospitals and the roads. Also in a lot of Italian supercars. I'm not asking for those.

When I became Australian, I assumed a country this rich in gas and minerals would have the same to show for it. Then I looked at the numbers.

We ship about as much gas as Qatar. Our gas profits tax raised about $51 each.

That was 2025-26.7,28,29 The beer tax raised more.7 And when people get angry about housing, politicians point at migrants like me instead of the tax settings they wrote. I want less migration too, matched to the homes we actually build. It's just not the whole fix.

I'm not asking for Qatar. Qatar keeps its gas money for a few hundred thousand citizens, a small minority of the people who live there. There are 27.9 million of us.29,30 I'm asking for Norway, a democracy that taxes petroleum profits at 78% and has saved about $600,000 for every person in the country.20,31,32,33,34 We've saved $10,400.21

That gap isn't bad luck. Both major parties chose it, again and again, and then defended it. I chose this country. I want it to get a fair price for what it sells.

Dear homeowner: this isn't about you. It's about the rules.

If you own the home you live in, a higher price mostly lives on a property website. Sell to move, and the next home has gone up too, plus stamp duty.105 There's nowhere cheaper to go, either: by September 2025 a regional home cost 8.1 times income, against 8.2 in the capitals.22

The gain turns into spending money only if you sell and buy less, or borrow against it. That means downsizers, people moving overseas, investors selling to the next buyer, and heirs. The Productivity Commission says people inherit at about 50 on average, which is a long wait for a deposit.106

The bill goes to whoever buys next: the young, people who came here to build a life, and our own kids. They're bidding against people who already own and bring their gains with them. Existing owners take about 65% of new owner-occupier home loans.22 Home ownership among 30-to-34-year-olds fell from 64% in 1971 to 50% in 2021.107

Our plan

This time, we're setting a reserve.

Charge for the gas. Build the homes. Bank the rest. Some of this the Greens want, and some of it the Coalition wants.95,108 We want all of it, and we answer to neither.

Tax gas profits properly.

Norway's tax rate on petroleum profits is 78%.31 By the Superpower Institute's estimate, Australia takes about 18% of the gas industry's cash flow in tax, against more than 75% elsewhere.8 A 25% levy on gas exports would raise about $17 billion a year, on the Australia Institute's estimate.109

That's more than six times the beer tax.

House everyone first.

122,494 people were homeless on Census night 2021.23 Housing them comes before any tax cut.

Stop making Australians pay a fortune to stay in Australia.

If every home doubles, a family that stays put gets a bigger number, not a bigger house. Their next home doubled too, and so did their kids' first one. In the five years to September 2025, home values rose 53.5% and household incomes 20%.22 Finish the job on negative gearing and the capital gains discount, with no carve-out for investors who already own.71 Build public and community housing, and don't stop. If you've just bought, we're not out to crash your price. We want house prices to stand still while incomes catch up.

Match migration to homes.

Set each year's intake by the number of homes finished the year before. Nobody should arrive in a country with nowhere to live, and nobody already here should be left without a home either. The Coalition now promises a cap like this too. It also promises to bring back the investor tax breaks. We wouldn't.93,95

Bank it, and pay every Australian a dividend.

Put the money in a national wealth fund, as Norway does, and pay a yearly dividend from it, as Alaska has paid its residents since 1982. In 2026 it was US$1,200 a person, including a US$200 energy relief payment.110

Build things again.

We mine about a third of the world's lithium.111 Refine it here. Make our own fuel. Train our own engineers and tradies, and give them something to build.

Keep the next Bluey.

When public money helps make something, the rights stay here. The same goes for the next port and the next lab.

Legalise cannabis. Then actually tax it.

Let adults grow and buy it legally. The ACT has allowed home growing since 2020.112 The Parliamentary Budget Office costed the Greens' plan for a national legal market at an $8.7 billion budget gain over the 11 years to 2035-36.108

The first crop we'd charge properly for.

A country that ships about as much gas as Qatar shouldn't have 122,494 people homeless. That was the count on Census night 2021.23,28
